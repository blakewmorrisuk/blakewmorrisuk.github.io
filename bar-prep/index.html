Bar Prep App

May 18, 2026 — running on my iPhone

Building an app to help me study for the bar after my studies in the evening. Will publish in the appstore after the bar in early August.

It's a multi-platform iPhone, iPad, and Mac app I'm using to track my Barbri summer plan day-by-day. Tasks, notes, flash cards, and a session log for Adaptibar practice. The lock-screen widget counts down to the exam, and I can mark tasks done from anywhere via Siri Shortcuts.
