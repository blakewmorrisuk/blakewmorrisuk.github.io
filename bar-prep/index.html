Bar Prep App

May 18, 2026 — running on my iPhone

Building an app to help me study for the bar after my studies in the evening. Will publish in the appstore after the bar in early August. The teaser below is a live mini-version with sample data so you can poke around without an iPhone.

9:41 ••• ◴ ▮

Tap Enter, then poke around. The countdown is real (live, to July 28). Tasks toggle, cards flip. Sample data only.

The real app is multi-platform (iPhone, iPad, Mac) and syncs the full week board, notes, flash cards, and Adaptibar session log. The lock-screen widget counts down to the exam, and Siri Shortcuts let me mark tasks done or log an Adaptibar session from anywhere.

Once the bar is behind me, I'll de-personalize it (right now my Barbri schedule is hardcoded), add iCloud sync across devices, polish the UI, and ship it. Target: August 2026.

P.S. The very last thing on the build list is Pixel Pals: tiny pixel-art companions inspired by the Study Buddies the UKY Rosenberg School of Law Library puts out every finals season. If you don't see them on the home screen, the app isn't finished yet.
